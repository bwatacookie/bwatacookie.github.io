Games

Ball Journey

Arcade One tap Endless

One tap reverses your ball. That is the whole control scheme — and it is all you get while asteroids rain down, corridors squeeze shut and the screen speeds up around you. Graze a hazard instead of dodging it and your combo multiplier climbs, so the safest run is never the best one.

Pixel Breakers

Casual Brick breaker Pixel art

Aim, release, and a whole volley of balls tears up the board, ricocheting until the last one drops. Then every brick shoves down a row — and if one ever reaches your launcher line, the run is over. The volley keeps growing as you go, so a deep run turns the screen into a storm of bouncing pixels.

Wordlike

Word roguelike English · French Endless

Spell the best word you can on a line of bonus slots and hit the score goal before you run out of plays. Every win grows an endless path with a boss every fifth stop, merchants and chests along the way. Buy relics, engrave your letters and take on the daily challenge, the same for every player.

Studio

Bwatacookie Studio is a one-person operation making arcade games for Android and iOS. No publishers, no roadmaps written by committee — just small games shipped when they actually feel good to play.

Ball Journey is built in Flutter and Flame, Pixel Breakers and Wordlike in Unity — whichever engine suits the game, rather than one house tool forced onto both. Progress, coins and settings stay on your device.
