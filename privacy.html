Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications and games published by Bwatacookie Studio ("we", "us", "our"), including but not limited to Ball Journey and Pixel Breakers. By using any of our apps, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

We do not collect or store personal information on our own servers. Our apps may use the following third-party services, which collect data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Games Services — used for sign-in, achievements, and leaderboards. When you connect your Google account, your public Play Games profile (player ID, display name, avatar) and game progress are shared with Google.
  • Google AdMob — used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect advertising identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase — used to process in-app purchases. Payment information is handled entirely by the respective app store and is never seen or stored by us.

Locally Stored Data

Game progress, settings, and in-game currency are typically stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us.

Children's Privacy

Our apps do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising is served in accordance with Google's and Apple's applicable policies for child-directed treatment where relevant.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information with third parties beyond the service providers listed above, which process data under their own privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. This single policy applies across all of our current and future apps unless a specific app states otherwise.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at bwatacookie@gmail.com.
